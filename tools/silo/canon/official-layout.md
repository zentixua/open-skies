# Silo layout from the production's own material (12.11.2026)

Source files are on the laptop in /mnt/data/projects/incoming/mod/run/silo-research/official/ (INDEX.md). The original of this file is in the laptop worktree at .claude/worktrees/gallant-rhodes-08f657/mod/run/silo-research/official/LAYOUT.md.

Frame name prefixes: `bfdg/` = frames/bfdg-designing-the-silo/; `ew/` = frames/extending-worlds/ (S2 sets of Silos 17 and 18). ArtStation paths are artstation/<artist>/<id>/<image>.

Sizes marked ≈ are estimates against a named scale object in one image, with about ±25 % perspective error. "Unknown" means no image or statement settles it.

## Hard numbers stated by the production
- 144 levels, "a mile deep", 10,000 people (BFDG 7:19–7:22; Gold Derby 2:01). Bocquet says "5,000" once (LRM 11:40).
- Shaft stage: an old cold store with about 40 ft (12.2 m) clear height, enough for **two levels** of the shaft (BFDG 28:10–28:27; Gold Derby 3:21–3:24 "two levels of balconies").
- They built 270° of the shaft ring on two levels; the fourth quarter was green screen (BFDG 30:14–30:26; Gold Derby 4:08).
- Generator room: a 120° "cake slice" of a round room was built (LRM 9:16–9:19).
- About 70–72 sets (Qv1k 3:25, 4:09; Gold Derby 2:41). Cornfield: 1,500 stalks, about 2–3 tennis courts (Qv1k 15:10–15:33).
- **Not stated anywhere:** shaft diameter, gallery width, floor-to-floor height, cafeteria size, storeys per level.

## 1. Shaft (atrium), stair, landings, bridges
**Shown:** bfdg/t2014s (Bocquet slide "SILO/WOOL – STAGE A – SHAFT LAYOUT": a plan and two renders), t1898s (white stage model), t1764s, t1923s and t1943s (shaft clips), t2163s and t2164s_91 (the built set), t2240s (the dome "at top of SHAFT for Main Lighting").

**Concepts:** Downman oby0Dk/52–53 (municipal), 24–27 (ceiling); Valette 39xAqA (early alternatives).

**Final S1 build, from the plan in t2014s:**
- A round open shaft.
- In its centre a solid round concrete core, with the spiral stair wound round its outside (solid parapet, double handrail).
- A **ring gallery** runs round each level. Its inner edge is scalloped into shallow bays between big round concrete columns, which rise through the levels with ringed light collars.
- **Two straight radial bridges**, about 135° apart, join the gallery to a landing ring round the stair core on each level. The stair turns between floors and lands on that ring.
- Behind the gallery are the level's facades (section 2).
- Built: 270° of gallery on two levels, with the stair core over one of the stage's own columns (BFDG 33:37–34:08).
- Top of the shaft: a dome of oval coffers with a central lighting rig (t2240s; t2170s Freedom Day).

**Early concepts not built:** 39xAqA 01–12 (three bridges and a hexagonal core; an X-shaped void), 15–16 (18 radial bays and three bridges, 22 Jan 2021), 17–20 (stacked straight flights). A double helix was dropped (BFDG 26:32).

**Sizes (≈):**
- Gallery walkway ≈ 3–4 m (t2163s, person 1.7 m).
- Stair core ≈ 2.5–3 m across; the flight ≈ 1.8–2 m wide; parapet ≈ 1.1 m.
- Shaft diameter: unknown in metres. In the plan the stair-core circle is ≈ 1/5 of the open shaft, and the gallery depth ≈ 1/6 of the ring's outer radius. There is no scale bar.
- Floor-to-floor: unknown. Bound: two levels fit inside the 12.2 m stage together with the top gallery's soffit and lights, so **≲ 6 m per level** (BFDG 31:00–31:13: "dependent to the inch").

## 2. Typical level: galleries, facades, alleys, apartments
**Shown:** oby0Dk/52–54 (municipal); bfdg/t1923s, t1943s, t2163s; alley plan bfdg/t3452s; S2 alleys ew/t0600s, t0660s, t0676s and sheets 09–11; Valette Dvb9q0/01 (alley market); apartments bfdg/t3391s–t3433s, oby0Dk/21–23.

- **Gallery facade:** the shaft-side wall of each level shows **two storeys** above the gallery floor.
  - Ground storey: doors, shop fronts and round-cornered windows.
  - Upper storey: small windows, balconies and bay boxes.
  - Above that, the next gallery's soffit with strip lights.
  - Public buildings get a big plaque panel over a double door with steps ("Department of Parks and Recreation").
- Everything is round: doors, windows and "racetrack" portals (Tyldum, Inside Look 0:30; Gold Derby 6:26–6:40).
- **Alleys** run back from the gallery through openings in the facade.
  - S1 alley stage: a figure of eight round a central block, with a stair up at one end. It was joined to the shaft stage and redressed as different parts of the silo (t3452s; BFDG 31:45–31:55, 57:33).
  - S2 alleys: 2-storey frontages, stoops of 3–4 steps, balconies, AC boxes and lanterns; a lattice skylight ceiling (t0676s); an oval portal at a junction (t0600s).
  - Alley width ≈ 3–5 m. Frontage ≈ 2 storeys, ≈ 6–7 m to the ceiling (t0660s, door 2 m).
- **Apartments:** modular pods (a lobby plus bolt-on rooms), rounded corners and a round door. Five basic plans became about 20 (Qv1k 5:07–5:32; BFDG 56:37–57:08).
- Unknown: alleys per level, their depth, and whether every level has them.

## 3. Level 1 cafeteria, sheriff station, holding cell
**Shown:** bfdg/t2338s (slide "CAFETERIA – Option 03"), t2375s (model), t2423s (full-size portal mock-up), t2554s, t2586s, t2643s (the built set); ew/t1232s–t1290s; police bfdg/t2650s–t2665s and ew/t1296s–t1360s; holding cell bfdg/t2756s–t2785s; oby0Dk/30–32.

- **Shape:** one round room. The flat ceiling has concentric rings and radial beams round a central flower-shaped light. There are **no columns** in S1, because Tyldum didn't want any (BFDG 39:19–39:31); the cafeterias further down do have columns (BFDG 43:55). Cafeterias exist "all the way down" (BFDG 38:40, 42:40).
- **Screen:** a long curved screen with oval ends, set into the outer wall, on the far wall opposite the entrance ("coming in you can see the big screen on the far wall", BFDG 40:34).
- **Entrance:** a big oval portal, with a few steps down into the room. Option 03 has two entrances on opposite sides, the serving counter along the wall between them, and the screen on the remaining arc. On set the cafeteria was not joined to the shaft; VFX joins them (BFDG 32:32). Whether the bridge lands right at the portal is unknown: in t1266s the portal opens onto a flat landing with apartment doors.
- **Size (≈):** the room is 25–30 m across (t2375s: about 10 tables of 6 across), with 60–70 tables. Ceiling ≈ 4–5 m.
- **Sheriff station:** a round office with concentric circles in the floor and desks round the middle; barred cells along one wall. It is "in the top" and shares its floor and ceiling with the cells (BFDG 44:08–44:31). Its position relative to the cafeteria is unknown.
- **Holding cell:** a tiled corridor cell behind the vault-like airlock door, then the ramp up to the exit (BFDG 46:23–46:50). Door ≈ 2.4 m.

## 4. IT
**Shown:** Valette qek8GD/01, 03 (Bernard's office); S2 ew/t1008s–t1072s (IT office), t1080s–t1100s (fan room, vault door), t1114s–t1150s (server room), t1186s–t1230s (vault library); Downman kNGoJx (IT freezer pantry, never built); oby0Dk/60–63 (watchers control room).

- **Bernard's office:** rectangular, with blue walls, two round concrete columns, a central double door and oval shelf niches.
- **Corridors:** curved, blue-panelled, with wooden doors (ew t986s–t1006s).
- **Server room:** circular, with tiered stepped racks round the wall, a ring light in the ceiling and a raised dais on the far side (ew t1126s).
- **The Vault:** a heavy round door off a fan room. Behind it, a tall book-lined library with spiral stairs and a hanging orrery (ew t1200s). Whether this is Silo 17's or 18's is unknown.
- **Bullpen:** "one set we had to have 40 desks" (BFDG 51:12), but it isn't shown, so its layout is unknown.
- **The "Algorithm" computer:** not in this material.
- IT, the market and the cafeteria were built in turn inside one shell under one ceiling (BFDG 39:55–40:17).

## 5. Market
**Shown:** Valette Dvb9q0/01; ew/t0414s–t0560s; ew/t0680s–t0758s (Silo 17 junk market). Narrow alleys with stalls in front of the 2-storey frontages, awnings and lamps. A dedicated market hall: unknown.

## 6. Farms
**Shown:** Valette 1x3gyL/01–09; oby0Dk/03–08; Downman oJ9lqJ (Silo 17).
- A farm level spans the full width of the silo (Gold Derby 8:25). It is one tall open storey round the shaft, behind a ring of columns, with the stair and gallery passing through it and water towers in the fields (1x3gyL/01, 05).
- Hydroponic racks are in separate rooms.
- The storey is ≈ 6–8 m tall. Which levels: unknown.

## 7. Medical
**Shown:** oby0Dk/50 and 53 ("MEDICAL" over an oval glazed entrance off the gallery); oby0Dk/36, 51; ew/t0804s–t0934s.
- A corridor with doors on both sides, ≈ 2.5–3 m wide.
- A nursery behind a long oval window.
- Level: unknown.

## 8. Supply
Downman a0gbaR (S2 "Critical Supply"): entered from the shaft landing through big doors marked "M-14"; a racked warehouse under the same flower-shaped light.

## 9. Mechanical, generator, digger void
**Shown:** Valette QXD6Yr/01–11 (generator, with a clip), P6yyRo/01–10 (digger void); ew/t0144s–t0346s; trash chute ew/t0108s–t0142s.
- **Generator room:** round, with a huge central generator, red-railed catwalks across pits, and a control room with a long window up a stair on the far side. The outer corridor, the room and the control room were one set (LRM 9:16–9:52).
- **Digger void:** a tall rock cleft with walkways and ladders. The digger head is a round cutting face. Size unknown.

## 10. Silo 17 (S2)
The same architecture, ruined: overgrown or flooded alleys and farms (ZldXm8, ZldBeR, oJ9lqJ), the suit room (K3zd19), judicial holding (XJdYq3). Murals tell 17 from 18 (P6256o).
