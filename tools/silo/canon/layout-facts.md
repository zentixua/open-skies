# Layout and scale facts said in the show (dialogue) and where they come from

Every line here is quoted or paraphrased from the episode dialogue (`transcripts/sXXeYY.txt`), unless marked otherwise.
Use these before any estimate. Frame-measured numbers and production-set numbers are in `../official-layout.md`,
`../locations/` and `../../scale-review.md`; this file is what the characters say.

## For the 25567 build (what is new versus `final-1to1-proposal.md`)
- Seven groups around Silo 1, each fed by its own Safeguard trunk line, matches the plaque's 7 clusters and the hub lines.
- Silos are not all oriented the same way: Silo 17 is "turned" relative to 18 (cameras face different directions).
- The silo has a ramp the residents use to go out and back in (S3E10) and a berm that blocks the view to the other silos of
  the group; outside you can see over it from the top of the berm.
- Silo 1 differs: an elevator instead of the stair, unlit storage levels, cryo bays low, Operations and cafeteria at the top.
