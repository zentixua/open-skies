# S3E09 "Farewell": the surface on opening day (2052), frame by frame

Collected 12.11.2026 for the opening-day scene at 25567. Sources: Russian fan wiki silo.fandom.com/ru, 1920×802 episode
frames `3х09_*` (fetched through the MediaWiki API); tvobsessive.com S3E9 recap screenshots (about 800 px wide);
one S3E10 frame (`3х10_*`). Plot facts are from the RU wiki page "Прощай".

How the sizes were measured: pixels against a person (1.7 m), a coach (12 m), or the outer pale ring path, which the
canon fit puts at 128 m across (`tools/silo/canon_layout.json`, `edge_source`). Every size has its scale object and an
error range. **Uncertain** marks anything the frame does not settle. Perspective is the main error: an object nearer or
farther than its scale object changes the answer, so take ±30 % unless a line says otherwise.

## What the plot says about the places (RU wiki "Прощай")
- Daniel and Helen arrive **by car**. Helen is sent on a tour of **Silo 18**, with a group led by the guide Everett.
- **The stage stands next to Silo 1** ("сцена, расположенная возле Укрытия 1"). Peter Gabriel plays, Rosalind speaks.
- Daniel walks off and **climbs the hill** for phone signal. Helen gets out of Silo 18 the same way, climbs **to the top of
  the hill** and waves to him. Then the flash, the mushroom, and a security man tackles Daniel; **they roll down the
  hill**. With the blast wave coming, Daniel and Henry **run into Silo 1**.
- On screen the "hill" is a ring berm (frames 10, 23, 25).

## Whole complex
**01-aerial-complex.jpg**: high oblique of the whole complex. The parking loop and gate are at the top, which our
layout calls north.
- Each silo is a round **bowl inside a berm ring**. A darker band is the berm's inner slope. **A thin pale path circles
  outside each berm**; that circle is the 128 m scale. The berm crest ring measures about 0.70 of the path circle
  (≈ 90 m) on Silo 1 (frame 03).
- Pale paths about 1–3 px wide **link the rings into a network**: tangent arcs between neighbours, plus straight
  spokes. **The straight central avenue** runs from the gate to Silo 1's ring path, and continues south of Silo 1
  to the far edge.
- Most rings show **one small pale dot inside the bowl**, off-centre, and **one short radial pale line across the
  berm**. These are probably the entrance and its path, but that is **uncertain at this resolution**. The line's
  direction differs from ring to ring.
- **Rosette discs** (frame 04) are small round plots with **4–6 concentric dark rings inside** and white specks
  (stones or people, **uncertain**). Each has its own pale path circle, and each sits in a three-cornered gap between
  silo rings.
  - Size: the outer circle measures 57 px against 145 px for the neighbouring silo's path circle, so ≈ **50 m
    across** (45–55 m).
  - Some smaller plain discs of ≈ 40 m look flat, with faint rings at most.
  - Visible: about 5 concentric ones and 2 plain ones in this frame.
- Surroundings: forest all round the field and a few ponds.
  - North-east: white barrel-roofed hall buildings and a large car park.
  - South-west and south-east corners: sheds, round hangars and white storage tanks.
  - A railway along the north-west edge.
  - Tall thin masts (light or radio) along the field edges.

**03-aerial-silo1-zoom.png** (frame 01, ×5 with pixel ruler): **Silo 1 on opening day.**
- A **cluster of 5 white tents** sits in the north half of the bowl: one larger in the north (≈ 8 m) and four smaller
  ones (west, south-west, south-east, east) on a ring of ≈ 11 m radius round a dark centre. (Recounted on the zoom:
  an earlier version of this note said six.) A second cluster of **3 small white tents/objects** stands to
  the west.
- **A straight pale path** runs from the tent cluster **due south** across the berm. There it meets a **pale bare
  apron**, ≈ 50 m wide along the ring path (gravel or trodden earth, **uncertain**), and goes on south.
- The avenue from the gate ends on the ring path at the north. **No path crosses the north berm** at this
  resolution.
- **Uncertain** which white shape is the stage itself.

**02-aerial-parking-gate.jpg**: arrival, looking along the axis towards the field.
- The access road comes up through forest. It has **two lanes each way with a yellow centre line**, and widens to
  3–4 lanes each way with grass medians.
- It ends in a **loop round an oval lawn about 7 coach lengths across (≈ 85 m)**.
- About **35 coaches** and a few cars are parked on both sides of the loop.
- Gate:
  - a **white tensile canopy with 5–6 peaks**, on low arches;
  - in front of it, a dense crowd on a forecourt;
  - on both sides, a **long low grey band** (wall or fence, **uncertain**);
  - white tents to the left and right of the forecourt.
- Beyond the gate, the straight avenue leads into the field with a stream of people. The field is flat grassland with
  hedgerows and the forest edge beyond.

## Inside a ring (Silo 18) and the entrance
**05-path-inside-silo18-bowl.jpg**: a drone looks down into Silo 18's bowl.
- The floor is level mown grass. The berm all round is a smooth grassed bank (height: see frame 10).
- **One pale path** (light beige, compacted fine gravel) crosses the bowl. Two people walk side by side on it with
  room to spare, so it is ≈ **2.5–3 m wide**. Its end widens to a **rounded pad ≈ 1.5× the path width**, in the
  open bowl.
- No ramp opening is visible at the pad. Whether the stair is there or hidden is **uncertain**.
- On the far berm foot stands a **light concrete block** ≈ 3 m wide and ≈ 2 m tall (people beside it are 35–40 px
  tall). This is the camera block of frame 07 seen from behind, **likely, not proven**.
- Visitor groups of 6–20 stand around the bowl.
- Beyond the berm: flat ground with scattered trees and many tall thin masts on the horizon.

**06-ramp-rim-crowd.jpg**: the tour group steps down into the entrance.
- At ground level the opening is a **slot between two low concrete curbs** flush with the lawn. The curb top is ≈ 0.4 m
  wide and stands ≈ 0.2 m above the grass (50 px against a 455 px, 1.78 m man at the same depth).
- The slot's near end has a **chamfered (cut) corner**. The concrete is smooth light grey.
- Behind: the berm, ≈ 3–4 people high from here; a guide in mustard; groups queued in lines.

**07-camera-block-and-stair-2052.jpg**: Everett at the stair head.
- In front is the **stair opening**: a trench with smooth light-grey concrete parapets, rounded tops and rounded
  corners, and steps going down.
- Behind it stands the **camera block**:
  - a flat roof slab with a rounded edge, ≈ 0.3–0.4 m thick, cantilevered over a **recessed dark grey back wall**;
  - a thick light concrete side wall with a sloped outer face;
  - in front, on a **3-step plinth** (≈ 0.45 m in all), a **truncated concrete cone** ≈ 1.3 m wide at the base and
    ≈ 1 m tall, topped by a small **bronze or brass dome sensor** (≈ 0.2 m).
- Height to the roof top ≈ 2.4–3 m. It is ≥ 3.4 m wide; Everett hides the right end. Scale: the guide standing beside
  it, ≈ 260 px for 1.65 m.
- The bowl floor around it is flat lawn, and the berm crest rises behind.

**08-camera-block-present-day.jpg**: Bernard outside Silo 18 in the present.
- The **same block** (roof slab, dark recess, side walls, stepped plinth) is weathered brown-grey on dead, dry ground.
- A **red light** shows in the middle, at the sensor's place.
- This confirms that the block is the camera housing the silo sees through.

**09-ramp-tunnel-inside.jpg**: Helen walks up out of the entrance; this is the ring interior just below ground.
- **At the top it is an open trench** (grass at ground level, top left), then a **roofed tunnel** going down.
- Walls: **vertical fluted (ribbed) concrete panels** with **narrow vertical white light slots** between the panels,
  one per panel. The walls meet the floor in a **smooth curved cove**.
- Ceiling: **faceted (polygonal)**, made of ribbed grey metal panels.
- Floor: light concrete with **transverse grooves**.
- A **galvanised steel handrail** on brackets runs along the right wall. The open part's wall top carries a steel
  rail with angled brackets.
- Tunnel width is **not measurable** from this frame: Helen is well in front of the portal.

**10-stair-head-bowl-berm-tree.jpg** (tvobsessive): from inside the bowl, across the stair head to the berm.
- The **stair head** has concrete parapets and steps going down. The bowl floor is flat lawn.
- The **berm** is a smooth grassed bank, ≈ **4–5 m above the bowl floor**. The scale is the woman standing on the
  crest; this is **uncertain**, because the berm foot is nearer to the camera than its crest.
- **A single round-crowned tree** stands on the crest, another tree to the left, and shrubs.

**27-s3e10-silo17-ramp-mouth-top-down.jpg** (S3E10, present day, Silo 17): people pour out of the entrance.
- Seen from above, the mouth is a **long rectangle with rounded corners**, framed by a concrete rim ≈ 0.3–0.4 m wide.
- Inside it are **5–6 people abreast**, so the inner width is ≈ 3–4 m. **Uncertain**: people are not shoulder to
  shoulder.
- The visible length is about 2.2 widths before the roofed part starts.
- The ground round it is bare, stony and dead. This contradicts a 7 m ramp only if the stair is narrower than the
  ramp; **the frames do not show the ramp's full width**.

## Stage, tents, crowd area (at Silo 1)
**11-stage-front.jpg**: the stage, with crew setting chairs.
- **Portal of bent dark-brown wooden ribs**: about 10 per side, springing from the stage floor and curving inward to
  meet overhead.
- Over it, a **white fabric roof on silver aluminium box-truss** towers at both sides. A lighting truss carries moving
  heads and a row of LED blinders.
- Behind: a warm-lit curtain and three **light-wood vertical-slat back panels**. The middle one carries the **gold sun
  emblem (ring of linked figures) inside a ring**.
- A dark wood lectern with the emblem, and a keyboard stand.
- **Stage floor ≈ 0.9 m high**: 5 steps up at each side, with slatted light-wood stair cheeks.
- Front: **planter boxes of light slatted wood** with pink and white flowers (lupin/foxglove-like) all along.
- **White vertical banners with the gold emblem** at both sides.
- Opening between the truss towers ≈ **14 m** (1490 px against 185 px for a 1.75 m person standing on stage).
- The ribs rise more than 6 m above the floor, past the top of the frame.
- Seats: rows of light grey-beige **folding chairs** on a pale (gravel or deck, **uncertain**) floor.

## The nuke and the wave
**24-mushroom-beyond-complex.jpg** and **24b-mushroom-foreground-brightened.png** (the lower band, ×1.5, brightened):
- A high view across the complex. **A berm ring has a gap**, and inside it are white **peaked tents and dark arched shapes**: likely Silo 1's bowl with the stage (**uncertain**: small and backlit).
- Pale paths wind across the field.
- **Far beyond the complex**, a mushroom cloud:
  - an orange-gold fireball cap;
  - a dark stem;
  - **thin horizontal condensation rings** across the stem and cap;
  - a broad, low **base surge** spreading along the horizon.
- The sky is orange.
- The burst is several km off; **no number can be taken from the frame**.

**25-mushroom-from-berm.jpg** and **25b-mushroom-from-berm-later.jpg**:
- A silhouette on the berm crest, with the mushroom straight ahead.
- **Bright cap with two flat condensation discs**, the stem in dust, and the base cloud rolling over the treeline.
- Shrubs on the berm. The whole scene is lit orange.

**26-blast-wave-dust.jpg**: the wave arrives at the stage.
- **Brown dust fills the air.** Hair and clothes blow hard.
- Truss and rigging lines lean and blur behind.
