# Concept study: Open Skies with Incoming at its heart

## What the pack is
Open Skies exists for [Incoming](https://github.com/zentixua/incoming): the pack is the world Incoming is played in.
Everything else serves it. Create Aeronautics, Immersive Aircraft and planes put players in the air, which is where
Incoming's strikes are seen and delivered from. Create builds the factories that make munitions. Towns, dungeons and
Voxy give the strikes places worth aiming at, and the distance to see them. The current set (pack-promo, 1.0.0) inverts
this: its icon is an airship with a mushroom cloud the size of a crumb on the horizon, its banner is a biplane over a
smoke column, and Incoming is one quarter of the feature strip. A player sees "an airship pack" and finds the mod
second.

## How packs built around one flagship mod brand themselves
| Pack | Flagship | What the pack's brand takes from the mod |
|---|---|---|
| Cobblemon Official Modpack (Modrinth, 11.5 M downloads) | Cobblemon | The mod's own mark redrawn as the pack icon (the hexagonal ball); name = mod name + "Official Modpack". No separate pack identity. |
| Create: Above and Beyond (simibubi, Create's author) | Create | Name prefix "Create:", Create's brass-and-cog look; the pack name is the subtitle. The "Create: …" pattern is copied by dozens of Create packs (Create: Astral, Create: Arcane Engineering, Cabricality "inspired by Create: Above and Beyond"). |
| Vault Hunters (Iskall85) | Vault Hunters mod | One brand for both: the pack *is* the mod's release vehicle; logo, art and trailer are the mod's. |
| Botania: Garden of Glass (Vazkii) | Botania | Lives inside the mod; its identity is Botania's flower and green. |
| Pixelmon Modpack | Pixelmon | Mod logo plus "Modpack". |

Observed rules: (1) the flagship's **signature motif** (Cobblemon's ball, Create's cog, Botania's flower) is the
pack's icon or sits at its centre; (2) the name either becomes "<Mod>: <Pack>" or carries an endorsement line
("Official Modpack"); (3) typography and palette come from the mod, so the two read as one family; (4) the pack keeps
one own element so it is not mistaken for the mod's page. Packs that only list the flagship among others (the
current Open Skies set) read as general packs.

## How far to lean on Incoming's identity
Incoming's identity, from its icon, banner and store kit (incoming#692):
- **Mark**: a pixel mushroom cloud on a night-navy sky over a burning horizon (32×32, 16 px cells).
- **Wordmark**: INCOMING in 9-row pixel letters, 2-cell strokes and gaps, cream `#eee5d6`.
- **Palette**: night navy `#1a2240` → dusk violet `#43304e`, embers `#ee6a1c` / `#ffa640` / `#ffe2a0`, soot browns.
- **Tagline**: letter-spaced Barlow Condensed SemiBold, cream.
- **Motifs**: the strike itself (mushroom, fireball, blast wave), the missile trail, the Strike Designator
  (binoculars: olive body, green screen and cross), the sky the strike comes from.

The current set already shares the wordmark letters, palette and tagline font; what it lacks is the **motif**. The
variants below differ in how much of Incoming they take: from the mod's mark under a new sky (closest, "official
pack") to the pack's own scene told through one Incoming motif. All keep Incoming's letterforms and palette; every
one makes an Incoming motif the first thing seen at 64 px.

## Variants
- **A · Strike Sky**: Incoming's mushroom cloud is the icon; the pack's airship flies in front of it, lit by the
  blast. Banner: the same scene in game (airship at dusk, a real Incoming nuke behind it). Tagline "AN INCOMING
  MODPACK". The pack's flight and the mod's strike in one picture.
- **B · Official Pack**: Cobblemon's model. The icon is Incoming's icon unchanged under an open day sky (the pack's
  name drawn as a sky); the wordmark is a lockup, INCOMING on top in its own letters, OPEN SKIES under it. The
  strongest tie: anyone who knows the mod knows the pack at a glance.
- **C · Contrail**: the missile trail as the brand line. Icon: an Incoming cruise missile diving out of the dusk sky
  toward a village, its contrail across the icon; the airship watches. Banner: a real salvo's trails over the valley;
  the contrail runs under the wordmark.
- **D · Designator**: the player's view through the Strike Designator. Icon: the mushroom inside the binocular lens
  with the designator's green reticle. Banner: an in-game frame through the designator; reticle brackets frame the
  wordmark. "See far, strike far" ties Voxy's distance to Incoming.

Store-safe split (each variant): store icon and store banner are in-game frames only (Modrinth rule 6.2); the pixel
icon, wordmark and ornaments stay on GitHub and in the launcher (the `.mrpack` and Prism icon).
